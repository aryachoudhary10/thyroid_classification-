Figure 8. External (cross-domain) validation on TN5000, ConvNeXt-Tiny backbone, DER-MIL only -- MR-MIL and lesion-only were not evaluated on TN5000 with this backbone. (a) ROC-AUC on the class-balanced 250-image evaluation subset with 95% bootstrap CI, adapted with bounding-box masks. (b) TN5000 ships pixel-level masks for 0 of 5000 images, so the nominal "pixel-mask" adaptation run received identical bounding-box input to the "bbox" run; the two sessions are therefore two independent trainings of the same configuration, and their 0.0163-point spread is a direct, measured estimate of training-stochasticity noise on this 250-image subset rather than an effect of mask type.
