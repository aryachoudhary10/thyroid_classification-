Figure 6. Robustness of DER-MIL, ConvNeXt-Tiny backbone, ThyroidXL held-out test cohort, all evaluated on the single frozen predictor with no retraining. (a) ROC-AUC under progressively degraded segmentation masks, from ground truth to complete mask removal (delta from clean: -0.0018 at 5px dilation to -0.3463 with zeroed masks). (b) Effect of removing one frame per patient chosen by the model's own reliability score. On this backbone the direction is the OPPOSITE of the ResNet-50 result reported in the supplementary figure of the same name: removing the frame DER-MIL calls least reliable costs MORE ranking performance (ROC-AUC 0.9730 after removal) than removing a random frame (0.9856) or the frame it calls most reliable (0.9859) -- the wrong direction for an informative ranking, and inconsistent with the ResNet-50 result on the same test. (c) Within-patient shuffling of which mask is paired with which frame -- a shortcut-reliance probe -- changes DER-MIL's ROC-AUC by only -0.0067, comparable to MR-MIL (-0.0015) and the lesion-only baseline (-0.0130). (d) Frame-order permutation invariance, confirmed on 707 test patients.
